Rachel's Hens

In light of David and I getting married, I get to have a genuine reason for a winery day trip celebration.

So please join me for a day of fun, wine and food in the Yarra Valley on the 20th Feb 2027.

The theme for the day will be best colour. You can wear your favourite colour combo, your brightest colour or your favourite patterns, whatever will make you feel the happiest as we bus around.

The tour I selected is a winery, a brewery with food options for 99% of allergies and then another winery stop.

Cost will be $150 for the day and covers the bus costs, both winery tastings and the brewery stop for both drinks and lunch.

There will also be 2 pick up locations since I have people from both sides of the city. One pick up will be in the city itself and then one pick up around the Croydon area.

I will also be curating a playlist for the day so please let me know a song you would like added to the music shuffle. It doesn’t need to be a song you think I will like, just a song that you would like to hear on a wine day out.
